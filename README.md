# SolvingChess

## Exploring mathematical pathways toward solving chess

SolvingChess is a research project investigating whether deeper mathematical structures can reveal new approaches to understanding and potentially solving chess.

The goal is not simply to build a larger chess engine. Instead, this project explores whether chess has hidden structure that can be represented, compressed or understood through mathematics.

## Research Themes

### Higher-dimensional geometry
- 4D chess and hypercube representations
- n-dimensional board spaces
- geometric interpretations of movement
- symmetry groups and transformations

### Mathematical approaches
- Algebraic representations of board states
- Graph theory and state-space geometry
- Topological structures
- Invariants and conserved properties
- Alternative descriptions of the game tree

### Complex systems
- Stigmergic computation
- Emergent search strategies
- Distributed exploration
- Novel solver architectures

## Research Inspiration

Inspired by recent developments in mathematical treatments of chess-like systems and higher-dimensional geometry, including exploration of how four-dimensional mathematical structures may provide new perspectives on chess.

## Central Question

Can chess be solved by discovering its underlying mathematical structure rather than only searching its astronomical game tree?
