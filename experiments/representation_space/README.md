# Representation Space Experiments

This area tests the hypothesis that chess difficulty may be partly caused by representation choice.

## Phase 1: Build Alternative Representations

Implement:

- standard board coordinates
- graph representations
- symmetry reduced states
- geometric embeddings
- higher dimensional coordinates

## Phase 2: Compare Complexity

Questions:

- How many unique states remain after symmetry reduction?
- Can equivalent positions be automatically detected?
- Do mathematical signatures predict strategic similarity?

## Phase 3: Small Chess Universes

Begin with tractable systems:

- king versus king
- king and rook endgames
- reduced boards
- simplified piece sets

## Success Criteria

A useful representation should:

- preserve game meaning
- reduce redundancy
- reveal structure
- enable new algorithms
